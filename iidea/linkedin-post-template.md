# LinkedIn Post Template

## Hook
Start with a strong statement that creates curiosity.

Examples:
- "Most people learn Power BI the wrong way."
- "If you want to become a data analyst in 2026, read this."
- "Here is the roadmap to a $100K data career."

Keep the hook under 2 lines.

---

## Problem

Explain the common mistake or challenge people face.

Example:
Many aspiring data analysts spend months learning random tools without understanding the bigger picture.

---

## Explanation

Break down the concept clearly.

Use simple language and practical context.

---

## Framework / Steps

Provide a structured solution.

Example:

Step 1 – Learn Excel fundamentals  
Step 2 – Understand SQL  
Step 3 – Master Power BI  
Step 4 – Learn data modeling  
Step 5 – Explore data engineering basics

---

## Example

Provide a real-world example or scenario.

Example:
At Access Analytic, most finance dashboards rely heavily on proper data modeling.

---

## Key Takeaway

Summarize the main insight in 1–2 lines.

Example:
Tools don't make analysts valuable. Understanding data does.

---

## Call to Action

Encourage engagement.

Examples:

- Comment "DATA" if you want the roadmap.
- Follow Analytical Guy for analytics insights.
