# Content Creation Style

Most of my content teaches analytics and AI concepts.

Claude should structure content like educational frameworks.

## Preferred format for posts

1. Hook
2. Problem
3. Explanation
4. Step-by-step solution
5. Example
6. Key takeaway

## Example Hook Style

Good hooks:

- "Most people learn Power BI the wrong way."
- "If you want to become a data analyst in 2026, read this."
- "Here is the roadmap to a $100K data career."

Hooks must be:

- clear
- curiosity-driven
- short

## Teaching philosophy

My goal is to simplify complex ideas.

Always assume the reader is new to the topic.

Use analogies when possible.
